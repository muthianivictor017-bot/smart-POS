    ℹ️ What is eTIMS? electronic Tax Invoice Management System by KRA Kenya. When enabled, every sale generates a tax invoice transmitted to KRA with a CU (Control Unit) number.
    ⚠️ Important: In test mode, the system generates simulated CU numbers for demonstration. For production use, you need valid KRA eTIMS credentials and must disable test mode. Contact KRA or your eTIMS vendor for API access.
